CourseGraph backend logic

One pure function: plan = f(student, majors, edits). Same input, same output. Catalog: Grinnell 2026–27.

Recalculation pipeline · runs on every edit

Student database

status ∈ completed · in_progress · planned · withdrawn · failed. Planned requires a real section in the current or next term.

Tablet and node states

Node ✓ when the term is over and every counted tablet has its ✓. Edit mode (pencil): ⊖ delete and ⇄ swap; past and in-progress tablets are locked.

2 · Match: which tablets get an R

Max bipartite matching, best tier first (completed › in progress › planned › projected). One course fills one slot per major and may count for both majors. Unmatched slots become R tablets.

3 · Distribute: route, then reflow

The route term is the target. Each open slot goes to the earliest term at or after its target where prereqs are met in earlier terms, the course is offered and load stays ≤ 18. Longest prereq chain first.

Arrow layout · always visible, never overlapping

Each arrow spans node j..k. Interval partitioning puts it in the lowest lane free across that span, so arrows in one lane never overlap. Gutter height = lanes × 16 px, snapped to an 8 px grid; corners are right angles; ⚠ sits on the target node's top edge.

Hard rules from the catalog

  • 124credits to graduate, 2.00 GPA, tutorial, a major, residency
  • 48 / 92most credits counted from one department / one division
  • Cminimum grade for major credit; S/D/F never counts toward a major
  • 12–18credits per term; 16 is normal progress; over 18 needs Registrar approval
  • Wno credit, no GPA; repeat allowed only after D or F, second attempt counts
  • Sem 1 / 2tutorial in the first semester (C or better); FYE usually in the second
  • 8 / 2semesters expected; at most two off-campus semesters
  • CSroute from the two sample plans; one of MAT-208, CSC-208 or MAT-218, a math elective, ≥ 20 CSC credits at Grinnell
  • Math32 credits, ≥ 16 at 300-level, one 300-level sequence (313–314, 316–317, 321–322, 335–336)

Edge cases handled

Cross-listed courses count once (MAT/CSC-208). AP credit clears prereqs, counts for the major only when stated. Either/or prereqs. Study-away terms hold no Grinnell courses. Double-major overlap. Catalog-year changes (CS Fall 2027). Instructor consent becomes a warning, never a silent pass. Missing data reads as unknown, never as satisfied.
